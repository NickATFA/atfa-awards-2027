In the hall

Hall 5 is 4,136 m². The stands sit inside a clear 3 m fire lane, with the stage in one corner and the breakout rooms behind the operable wall.

South-east corner Demonstration & stage zone

A 5 × 5 m quadrant stage with 30 seats around the curved edge, main and delay speakers, and an AV and mix position.

680 m² Breakout & seminar

Behind the operable wall, next to the hall.

680 m² Catering & networking

Also behind the operable wall.

Main entrance A clear way in

The main entrance opens onto a clear arrival area, and a pedestrian route runs straight across the hall to the stage. Both are kept free of stands.

Open sides Know what faces the aisle

Every stand lists how many of its sides are open to an aisle, from one up to an island stand open on all four.
